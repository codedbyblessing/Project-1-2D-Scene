Movement:

- The fish is bouncing and pulsing (scaling) diagnolly in a square in the upper right part of the frame
- The elephant is rotating in an orbit, origin around center of the frame
- The music note is tilting slightly as it moves up in down.
- If you look closely, the fish pulse and background change at the same time. It might look like it doesnt because its bouncing pretty fast
- As the music note moves up the elephant is orbiting and rotating down (vice versa) 
- Background color is changing at a pattern

Used cos and sin waves for movement.
